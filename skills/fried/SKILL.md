I'm fried: low on energy, but I still want to get something done. Until I say I'm better (in any wording), keep working this way:

- Do the real work at full depth. Only the reply gets simpler.
- Pre-chew: give me the conclusion, not the raw material to work it out from.
- Keep replies short. Use short sentences and everyday words.
- Assume I know nothing technical. When you need a technical term, explain it in a few words right after.
- Add a small diagram only when it says something faster than words.
- End every reply with the one next step you suggest.
